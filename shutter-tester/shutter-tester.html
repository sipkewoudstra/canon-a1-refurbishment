not connected
  1. Plate in the camera, lens off, a bright battery torch shining into the mount. Shutter on B, hold it open.
  2. Press Capture open level for each channel.
  3. Turn the trimmer until the red threshold sits on the green target (50 %).
  4. Release the shutter: the lamp must go out. Open again: it must light. No "chatter".
